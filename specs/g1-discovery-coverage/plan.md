## Summary

Close the discovery honesty loop over the proven first-party observation
foundation (G0 `disc.*` substrate, g1-data-truth-extensions PROVEN dependence
vocabulary) as additive layers in the EXISTING packages `discovery-universe`
and `cheap-monitor` — no new package is needed:

1. **Versioned source profiles and entry provenance (FR-DISC-011)** — a
   `disc.disc_source_profiles` table keyed (source_id, profile_version)
   declaring coverage scope, rights basis, query/filter version,
   upstream-dependence disclosure, manipulation-label policy, and collector
   linkage; a `disc.universe_entry_provenance` table keyed by universe entry
   storing normalized identity, entry reason (`disc_entry_reason` vocabulary),
   coverage-scope ref, rights record, query/filter version, and upstream
   dependence at entry time. Append-only (extends `disc.refuse_mutation`),
   FK-linked to the proven `disc.discovery_universe_entries`.
2. **Coverage metrics engine (FR-DISC-012, FR-DISC-007)** — a deterministic
   `CoverageMetrics` module computing, per source/collector-scope/profile at an
   explicit `asOf` boundary through the proven `visibleAt` predicate: unique
   discovery yield, pairwise overlap and effective independent yield (multiplied
   by the proven dependence multipliers), first-seen lead/lag distribution,
   provider lateness, source coverage loss, extended-at-first-seen rate,
   identity-failure rate, unsupported-program exclusion count, and price
   extension at first system availability (from the proven economic
   observations substrate). Everything is derivable from persisted rows — no
   wall-clock, no guessing.
3. **Independent-universe recall estimation (FR-DISC-006, FR-DISC-010,
   FR-DISC-009)** — a `RecallEstimator` producing recall estimates only over
   named populations (§63.7 vocabulary), only on the three admissible claim
   bases (independent first-party observation, independent provider lineage
   via the proven dependence edges, valid known inclusion probabilities with
   a Horvitz–Thompson-style weighted estimator for probability-sampled
   universes). Structural self-recall refusal: a universe generated by the
   evaluated source can never establish that source's recall. Shared-upstream
   lineage forces a disclosed-dependency verdict, never an independent claim.
   Retrospective classification consumes the proven
   `classifyRetrospectiveMiss` seam and extends it with inclusion-probability
   weighting.
4. **Population-constraint layer (FR-DISC-013)** — collector gaps
   (`collector_gaps.recovery_status`), decoder pauses
   (`col.collector_decode_pauses.revalidation_state`), unverified program
   versions (collector scope manifests), and provider unavailability
   (`prov.prov_operations.health_status`) resolve into versioned population
   constraints (`disc.population_constraints`, `disc_constraint_kind` +
   `disc_constraint_effect` vocabularies) that downgrade or block the affected
   population claim. Effects: `NARROW_CLAIM` (report carries the constraint),
   `EXCLUDE_WINDOW` (affected windows cannot count toward claims),
   `BLOCK_CLAIM` (no report may publish for the affected population while the
   constraint stands). Constraints are never silently counted as negative
   discovery outcomes.
5. **Claim-language gate (FR-DISC-014)** — a `ClaimLanguageGuard` refusing
   full-market, all-Solana, and universal-recall claim language unless the
   cited manifest's population class and sampling contract substantiate it
   (typed refusal at the seam, before publication).
6. **Selective chain-access admission (FR-DISC-008)** — a
   `SelectiveChainAccessGate` admitting direct-chain/indexer access only
   against versioned, bounded declarations (`disc.chain_access_declarations`:
   purpose VERIFICATION or RETROSPECTIVE_BACKFILL, candidate/slot/count/time
   bounds, STRICT_FREE cost plane, protected-reserve compatibility).
   Unbounded declarations refuse; paid fallback refuses; widened bounds are a
   new declaration version, never a silent edit; consumption exceeding
   declared tolerance raises an incident (AC-229 discovery facet).
7. **Cheap-monitor provenance riders (FR-DISC-009/011 support)** — additive
   columns on `disc.cheap_monitor_rows` and `disc.promotion_decisions`
   linking monitor rows and promotion decisions to the population manifest and
   entry-provenance they were derived from, keeping the AC-112 bounded-batch
   and AC-113 replayable-promotion laws intact while making their coverage
   claims population-scoped.

Plus additive extensions: `packages/domain/src/disc.ts` (new vocabularies +
error codes), `packages/shared-schemas/src/disc.ts` (new Zod schemas imported
from domain), migrations `g1_disc_0001`/`g1_disc_0002` (additive), fixtures
under `tests/fixtures/disc/` (new modules), telemetry catalog extension, and
the manifest-owned AC suites extended in place under the facet convention.

Strictly read-only (INV-001): no trading, custody, wallet-signing,
private-key, or transaction-submission capability anywhere; chain access is
read-only enumeration/verification.

### `g1_disc_0001_source_profiles.sql`

```text
CREATE TABLE disc.disc_source_profiles (          -- FR-DISC-011, §63.2/§63.7
  source_id              text NOT NULL,
  profile_version        integer NOT NULL CHECK (profile_version >= 1),
  source_class           text NOT NULL CHECK (source_class IN (
                             'FIRST_PARTY_SUPPORTED_PROGRAM_EVENT',
                             'FREE_AGGREGATE_DISCOVERY',
                             'AUTHORIZED_LAUNCH_FEED',
                             'USER_WATCHLIST_OR_MCP',
                             'AUTHORIZED_SOCIAL_AGGREGATE',
                             'SELECTIVE_CHAIN_VERIFICATION',
                             'RETROSPECTIVE_UNIVERSE_ENUMERATION',
                             'STRATIFIED_UNIVERSE_SAMPLE')),
  coverage_scope         jsonb NOT NULL CHECK (jsonb_typeof(coverage_scope) = 'object'),
  rights_basis           text NOT NULL CHECK (rights_basis IN (
                             'FIRST_PARTY_COLLECTOR',
                             'AUTHORIZED_FEED_CONTRACT',
                             'FREE_PUBLIC_TERMS',
                             'USER_PROVIDED',
                             'EXCLUDED')),
  query_filter_version   text NOT NULL CHECK (length(query_filter_version) > 0),
  upstream_dependence    jsonb NOT NULL CHECK (jsonb_typeof(upstream_dependence) = 'object'),
  upstream_lineage_keys  text[] NOT NULL DEFAULT ARRAY[]::text[],
  manipulation_policy    text NOT NULL CHECK (manipulation_policy IN (
                             'LABEL_AND_RETAIN',
                             'LABEL_AND_DOWNWEIGHT',
                             'EXCLUDE_PAID_PLACEMENTS')),
  collector_scope_ids    text[] NOT NULL DEFAULT ARRAY[]::text[],
  effective_from         timestamptz NOT NULL,
  superseded_at          timestamptz,
  created_at             timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
  PRIMARY KEY (source_id, profile_version),
  CONSTRAINT disc_source_profiles_window CHECK (
    superseded_at IS NULL OR superseded_at > effective_from)
);

CREATE TABLE disc.universe_entry_provenance (     -- FR-DISC-011, per entry
  entry_id                     text PRIMARY KEY
                               REFERENCES disc.discovery_universe_entries(entry_id),
  normalized_identity_id       text NOT NULL CHECK (length(normalized_identity_id) > 0),
  entry_reason                 text NOT NULL CHECK (entry_reason IN (
                                   'FIRST_PARTY_SUPPORTED_PROGRAM_EVENT',
                                   'FREE_AGGREGATE_OBSERVATION',
                                   'AUTHORIZED_LAUNCH_NOTIFICATION',
                                   'USER_WATCHLIST_ADDITION',
                                   'AUTHORIZED_SOCIAL_MENTION',
                                   'SELECTIVE_VERIFICATION_HIT',
                                   'RETROSPECTIVE_ENUMERATION_HIT',
                                   'STRATIFIED_SAMPLE_DRAW')),
  coverage_scope_ref           text NOT NULL CHECK (length(coverage_scope_ref) > 0),
  rights_record                text NOT NULL CHECK (rights_record IN (
                                   'FIRST_PARTY_COLLECTOR',
                                   'AUTHORIZED_FEED_CONTRACT',
                                   'FREE_PUBLIC_TERMS',
                                   'USER_PROVIDED',
                                   'EXCLUDED')),
  query_filter_version         text NOT NULL CHECK (length(query_filter_version) > 0),
  upstream_dependence_disclosed jsonb NOT NULL CHECK (
                                   jsonb_typeof(upstream_dependence_disclosed) = 'object'),
  first_party_observed         boolean NOT NULL,
  recorded_at                  timestamptz NOT NULL DEFAULT now()
);

ALTER TABLE disc.cheap_monitor_rows               -- FR-DISC-009/011 riders
  ADD COLUMN population_manifest_id text,
  ADD COLUMN entry_provenance_id    text;

ALTER TABLE disc.promotion_decisions              -- AC-113 replay input grows additively
  ADD COLUMN population_manifest_id text;
```

## Verification strategy per acceptance criterion

| AC     | Facet this package owns                                        | Positive proof                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Negative proof                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AC-110 | per-source provenance (FR-DISC-011)                            | every fixture sighting persists full provenance (identity, entry reason, query/filter version, coverage scope, rights, upstream dependence); append-only provenance survives re-persist idempotently (AC-110 discovery facet describe-block) | negative: a sighting missing any FR-DISC-011 field is refused; editing/deleting provenance is refused by trigger; identity-unresolved entries cannot double-count (§63.12)                         |
| AC-111 | independent-universe classification (FR-DISC-006/010)          | retrospective samples classify NOT_DISCOVERED only with independent lineage or valid inclusion probabilities; sampled universe uses inclusion-probability weighting; evidence refs recorded (AC-111 discovery facet)                         | negative: shared-lineage sample yields DEPENDENT_DISCLOSED (never independent recall); self-recall attempt (evaluated source's own universe) is structurally refused; no-basis estimate is refused |
| AC-112 | population-scoped bounded batches (FR-DISC-009)                | 1,000 candidates in bounded batches where every batch descriptor and promotion decision carries its population manifest rider; O(batches) law re-proven (AC-112 discovery facet)                                                             | negative: a batch/promotion write without a population manifest rider is refused; a rider naming a nonexistent manifest is refused                                                                 |
| AC-113 | replayable promotion with coverage riders (FR-DISC-009/011)    | promotion replay from frozen inputs + manifest rider is byte-identical; policy bump changes decision deterministically (AC-113 discovery facet)                                                                                              | negative: replay with a mutated rider (different manifest) is refused as a different decision input; rider-less frozen-input replay fails                                                          |
| AC-224 | constraint honesty on collector gaps (FR-DISC-013)             | an OPEN collector gap over a fixture window resolves to a population constraint that EXCLUDE_WINDOWs the affected span from claims and the report discloses it (AC-224 discovery facet)                                                      | negative: publishing the population report while an unresolved BLOCK_CLAIM constraint stands is refused; a gap silently counted as a negative discovery outcome is refused                         |

## Material decisions (proposed ADR texts — bind future packages)

### ADR-1 — Additive provenance tables beside the proven G0 universe substrate

**Decision**: FR-DISC-011 provenance lands as NEW append-only tables
(`disc.disc_source_profiles`, `disc.universe_entry_provenance`) FK-linked to
the proven `disc.discovery_universe_entries`, never as ALTERed columns on the
G0 entry table. `disc.cheap_monitor_rows` and `disc.promotion_decisions` gain
nullable rider columns only (population manifest/provenance refs) — the G0
state machines and CHECK laws stay byte-identical.

**Why binding**: g1-outcome-evaluation and g1-objective-governance consume
discovery provenance for denominators and population claims; a mutated G0
entry table would rewrite served discovery history (INV-004) and fork the
point-in-time law. The rider-column pattern keeps AC-112/AC-113 replay laws
frozen while extending their claim scope.

### ADR-2 — Recall honesty is structural at the estimator seam

**Decision**: `recall-estimator.ts` refuses self-recall by construction: an
estimate whose evaluated source is a member of the generating universe's
`sourceIds` (or whose lineage shares an upstream key with it, via the proven
`source_dependence_edges`) can never produce `INDEPENDENT_ESTIMATE` — the
verdict vocabulary encodes `SELF_RECALL_REFUSED` and
`DEPENDENT_DISCLOSED` as first-class outcomes that still persist for audit.
Inclusion-probability weighting uses only the manifest's frozen
`selectionProbabilities` (Horvitz–Thompson-style), and an estimate without a
named population manifest is unrepresentable in the schema.

**Why binding**: g1-objective-governance's objective-integrity checks read
these verdicts; any consumer-side re-derivation of "independence" would fork
the FR-DISC-010 law. The refusal must exist where the estimate is born, not
where it is consumed.

### ADR-3 — Constraints are data, not metrics

**Decision**: collector gaps, decoder outages, unverified program versions,
and provider unavailability are resolved once into versioned
`disc.population_constraints` rows (kind × effect), consumed read-only from
the proven G0 collector/provider tables. Reports publish only through a gate
predicate that refuses while an unresolved `BLOCK_CLAIM` stands and carries
`NARROW_CLAIM`/`EXCLUDE_WINDOW` disclosures otherwise. Constraint states are
never folded into discovery-yield denominators as negative outcomes.

**Why binding**: §63.12's "constrain population claims rather than being
silently counted as negative outcomes" is a cross-cutting property; encoding
it as data lets g1-outcome-evaluation's censoring/maturity logic consume the
same rows instead of re-deriving availability semantics.

### ADR-4 — Chain access is admitted, bounded, versioned — never silently broad

**Decision**: every direct-chain/indexer operation runs against a versioned
`disc.chain_access_declarations` row with purpose, program set, and four
numeric bounds (candidates, slots/run, calls/day, window seconds),
STRICT_FREE cost classes only (`paid_fallback_allowed` is CHECK-false), and a
tolerance for AC-229 incident semantics. Consumption rows record actual usage;
exceeding tolerance records an incident and recomputes effective bounds.
Bound changes are NEW declaration versions — the gate refuses operations not
covered by a current declaration and cannot edit bounds in place.

**Why binding**: FR-DISC-008's "cannot silently become broad paid ingestion"
is an enforcement property, not a policy hope. Later packages (exploration,
outcome observation) that want chain access must enter through this seam; a
private unbounded accessor anywhere else is a violation.

### ADR-5 — No new package; additive depth in the two existing discovery packages

**Decision**: this package extends `@foresift/discovery-universe` (metrics,
recall, constraints, claim-language, chain-access, profiles) and
`@foresift/cheap-monitor` (population riders) rather than creating a third
discovery package. The `disc` migration family continues under the existing
family registration; the Drizzle mirror continues to exclude the `disc`
schema (parity gate enumerates `public` + `sig`), so zero
`packages/persistence/**` source writes are needed — only the central
expected-script registry test extension (plan-sanctioned scope exception).

**Why binding**: keeps the modular-monolith surface minimal (constitution
III), avoids a third workspace package whose only seam would be the same
database, and preserves the G0 ownership boundaries.

### ADR-6 — Claim-language refusal at the seam, before publication

**Decision**: `claim-language.ts` evaluates structured claim objects (not
free text): a claim declares its scope adjectives, and the guard refuses
`FULL_MARKET`/`ALL_SOLANA`/`UNIVERSAL_RECALL` unless the cited manifest's
population class is the evaluated source's own full-coverage class AND the
sampling contract establishes it (selection probabilities present and
exhaustive). Refusals are typed (`DISC_CLAIM_LANGUAGE_REFUSED`) and recorded
in the telemetry catalog.

**Why binding**: FR-DISC-014 must hold for every future surface (reports,
exports, agent outputs). A structured seam now means later milestones plug
their renderers into the same guard instead of re-implementing string
policing.
