## Package invariants (package-level cross-cut)

1. **Named-population law (FR-DISC-009)**: every coverage, recall, or
   missed-gem artifact names exactly one `CoveragePopulationManifest` (§63.7
   vocabulary). No default population exists; a report without a manifest
   reference is refused at the seam, never silently scoped.
2. **Claim-basis law (FR-DISC-010)**: a recall/missed-gem estimate is
   admissible only on (a) independent first-party observation, (b) independent
   provider lineage, or (c) valid known inclusion probabilities. The evaluated
   source's own universe can never establish its own recall (structural
   self-recall refusal). Shared upstream lineage forces a disclosed-dependency
   verdict, never an independent-recall claim (§63.9).
3. **Entry-provenance completeness (FR-DISC-011)**: every universe entry
   carries per-entry provenance (normalized identity, entry reason, coverage
   scope, rights, query/filter version, upstream dependence) and every source
   carries a versioned source profile. Missing provenance blocks claim
   support; provenance rows are append-only.
4. **Constraint honesty (FR-DISC-013)**: collector gaps, decoder outages,
   unverified program versions, and provider unavailability surface as
   population constraints (consuming the proven G0 collector state read-only).
   A report cannot be published as unconstrained while an unresolved
   `BLOCK_CLAIM` constraint stands; constraints are never silently counted as
   negative discovery outcomes.
5. **Claim-language law (FR-DISC-014)**: full-market, all-Solana, and
   universal-recall claim language is refused unless the cited manifest's
   population class and sampling contract (selection probabilities) substantiate
   it. Refusals are typed, at the seam, before publication.
6. **Selective chain access (FR-DISC-008)**: direct-chain/indexer access is
   admitted only with versioned, bounded scope (candidate/slot/count bounds,
   verification or retrospective-backfill purpose). Unbounded declarations are
   refused; there is no paid fallback; bounds cannot be silently widened — a
   changed bound is a new versioned declaration.
7. **Read-only law (INV-001)**: no trading, custody, wallet-signing,
   private-key, or transaction-submission capability anywhere in this package.
   Chain access is read-only enumeration/verification; no transaction
   construction or submission surface exists.
8. **Point-in-time + event-time law (INV-005/006, constitution V/VI)**: all
   metrics are computed at an explicit `asOf` boundary over `available_at`
   visibility (the single proven `visibleAt` predicate); backfilled entries
   keep original chain time with honest availability, never backdated.

## Acceptance-criteria file ownership (facet convention)

All ten assigned ACs (AC-110…113, AC-224…229) are shared across this package's
nine requirements and already have positive AND negative files authored by G0
packages and extended in place by g1-capacity-contracts (cost facets). This
package only ADDS describe-blocks to the existing files under the established
facet-scope header convention — never rewrites or weakens existing cases:

- AC-110/111/112/113 — discovery-honesty facet: per-source entry provenance
  (011), independent-universe classification (006/010), bounded batches with
  population provenance (009), replayable promotion carrying the population
  manifest reference (009/011).
- AC-224 — discovery facet: an open collector gap opens a population
  constraint that downgrades/blocks the affected population claim instead of
  counting as negative outcomes.
- AC-225 — discovery facet: a backfilled universe entry keeps its original
  chain-time coordinates and receives no availability earlier than real
  retrieval; replay before retrieval cannot see it.
- AC-226 — discovery facet: provider-lateness spans are decomposed per source
  against the earliest first-party observation for the verified collector
  scope.
- AC-227 — discovery facet: selective chain-access admission stays inside the
  declared bounds of the 30-day capacity replay; unbounded declarations are
  refused before activation.
- AC-228 — discovery facet: broad aggregate scan depth degrades before
  collector continuity; first-party events are never erased by provider
  outage (§63.12).
- AC-229 — discovery facet: selective chain-access consumption exceeding the
  declared tolerance raises an incident and recomputes admission bounds
  without silently consuming paid overage.

The collector-machinery facet of AC-224/225 stays with the collector owners;
the cost-capacity facets stay with g1-capacity-contracts (PROVEN). No case in
this package duplicates those facets.
